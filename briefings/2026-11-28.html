  1. Oldest organisation: The Central Commission for Navigation on the Rhine, created at the 1815 Congress of Vienna and based in Strasbourg, is generally considered the world's oldest international organisation still in existence.
  2. Universal Postal Union: Founded in Bern in 1874 as the General Postal Union, it created a single postal territory for member states and became a UN specialised agency in 1948.
