Geopolitics Facts – Oceans, Straits & Chokepoints

  1. Bab-el-Mandeb: the name means "Gate of Tears" in Arabic; the island of Perim splits the strait into a narrow eastern channel about 3 km wide and a wider western one.
  2. Montreux Convention (1936): governs the Turkish Straits, limiting the tonnage and time in the Black Sea of non-Black Sea powers' warships, and letting Turkey restrict passage in wartime.
  3. Sound Dues: Denmark charged tolls on ships passing the Øresund from the 15th century until the 1857 Copenhagen Convention ended them, in exchange for a one-off payment from maritime nations.
  4. Lombok Strait: a deep-water alternative to Malacca for very large ships, and it lies along the biogeographic boundary known as the Wallace Line.
  5. Northwest Passage: Canada regards its Arctic channels as internal waters, while the United States holds that they form an international strait – a dispute unresolved for decades.
